▾ Build the iOS Shortcut (one-time, 5 min)
What this does: One tap on your Apple Watch (or "Hey Siri, sync shred") pulls today's HealthKit data and opens this page with it pre-filled. Pure deep-link — no server, no API key.

Setup (iPhone):
  1. Open the Shortcuts app → tap + (top-right) → New Shortcut
  2. Name it: Sync SHRED
  3. Tap + Add Action and add these blocks in order:
    • Find Health Samples · type: Active Energy · today only · sum
    • Find Health Samples · type: Workout · today · sum Active Energy Burned
    • Find Health Samples · type: Step Count · today · sum
    • Find Health Samples · type: Body Mass · most recent · 1 result (Hume writes here)
    • Find Health Samples · type: Body Fat Percentage · most recent · 1 result (Hume writes here)
    • Find Health Samples · type: Lean Body Mass · most recent · 1 result (Hume writes here)
    • Find Health Samples · type: Body Mass Index · most recent · 1 result (Hume bonus)
    • Find Health Samples · type: Basal Energy Burned · today · sum (Hume measures BMR)
    • Find Health Samples · type: Resting Heart Rate · today · average
    • Find Health Samples · type: Heart Rate Variability SDNN · today · average
    • Text action — paste this template (drag the variables from above into each placeholder): 
      https://truwellai.github.io/apex-protocol-tracker/private/cut.html?ae=[ACTIVE]&wc=[WORKOUT]&steps=[STEPS]&w=[WEIGHT]&bf=[BODYFAT]&lbm=[LEAN]&bmr=[BASAL]&rhr=[RHR]&hrv=[HRV]
    • Open URLs — pass the Text from above
  4. Tap the settings icon → Add to Home Screen (or pin to your Watch face under Shortcuts complication)
Daily flow:
  1. Wake up · weigh in · workout
  2. Tap Sync SHRED on watch face or say "Hey Siri, sync shred"
  3. SHRED opens with today's numbers pre-filled · tap Apply · done.
Pro tip: Add a second shortcut block to silently log everything to a Notes file with date stamp — instant audit trail.

Don't want to build the Shortcut? Open Apple Health every morning, read the 6 numbers off the dashboard, type them above, hit Apply. Takes 30 seconds.

⚖️ Hume scale enable steps (one-time):
  1. Open the Hume Health app on your phone
  2. Profile → Connections (or Settings → Integrations)
  3. Tap Apple Health → enable ALL data types (weight, body fat %, lean mass, BMI, body water, BMR, visceral fat, bone mass)
  4. Step on the scale once with bare feet — first sync takes ~30 sec
  5. Open Apple Health → Body Measurements → confirm Hume readings appear
Hume-specific advantage: their BMR is bioelectrical-impedance derived (more accurate than Mifflin-St Jeor estimate). Once Hume is syncing, SHRED's Energy card will use the actual measured BMR instead of the calculation. Big win on cut accuracy.
